Jeffery's Adventure

Godot GDScript Game Development

Overview

Jeffery's Adventure started as my Capstone Project at Neumont College of Computer Science. Since completing the course, I've continued to update and expand the game, transforming it into something more than just a school project.

Game Description

In Jeffery's Adventure, players embark on an exciting journey through The Factory. He must fight through enemies to destroy The Factory at all costs. Enemeis get stronger as Jeffery evolves his weapons with parts from defeated bosses.

Features

  • Multiple pathways with increasing difficulty
  • Original game design and mechanics
  • Responsive controls and smooth gameplay

Technical Implementation

  • Built using the Godot Engine
  • Custom physics and collision systems
  • Efficient level design and implementation
  • Original artwork and animations

My Role

As the sole developer, I was responsible for all aspects of the game except for the music. This included:

  • Game design and mechanics
  • Programming and implementation
  • Level design and creation
  • Art and animation
  • Testing and optimization

Future Plans

I plan to continue working on and expanding Jeffery's Adventure. Future updates may include:

  • The Other Two Pathways
  • More challenging obstacles
  • Additional characters(?)
