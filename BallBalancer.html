Ball Balancer

Made in Godot over 3 weeks for the Boss Rush Game Jam 2025.
The theme of the Jam was "SPIN", so I made a game about
spinning evil balls (bosses) on your finger to destroy them.
The main gameplay mechanic is that you have to actually balance
the balls on you finger or it will fall of. Spinning makes it easier.
